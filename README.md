# 🚚 Swiftroute Logistics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Swiftroute Logistics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze logistics operations, including order processing, delivery performance, driver performance, hub efficiency, and vehicle utilization.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of logistics KPIs and helps identify operational trends, compare hub performance, monitor vehicle breakdowns, and support data-driven business decisions.

**Reporting Period:** December 2024

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall order volume and delivery performance.
- Analyze order processing and capacity across logistics hubs.
- Evaluate driver experience, ratings, and delivery activity.
- Track vehicle availability, maintenance, and breakdowns.
- Identify operational inefficiencies and potential areas for improvement.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations, if used in the project
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation, if used in the project

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Main Logistics Dashboard
- Displays key performance indicators for orders, on-time delivery, customer satisfaction, and delivery time.
- Compares order processing volume against hub capacity.
- Provides an overview of driver performance and vehicle status.
- Includes year and month filters for interactive analysis.

### 2. Drivers Overview
- Analyzes driver experience against performance ratings.
- Highlights drivers with the most deliveries during the selected period.
- Compares on-time delivery performance by employment type.
- Displays individual driver details and monthly order trends.

### 3. Hubs Overview
- Compares order processing volume and capacity across six hubs.
- Displays each hub's share of total orders.
- Compares hub performance scores, on-time delivery rates, and utilization.
- Provides a weekday-wise view of order processing time.

### 4. Vehicles Overview
- Compares total orders across vehicle models and vehicle types.
- Tracks active and maintenance vehicle counts.
- Visualizes breakdown counts by vehicle model and status.
- Helps identify vehicle models that may require closer maintenance monitoring.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard visuals for December 2024:

1. **Order Volume:** The dashboard records 1,224 total orders, providing a baseline for monitoring logistics activity.

2. **Delivery Performance:** The on-time delivery rate is 79.9%, indicating an opportunity to investigate delayed deliveries and improve delivery reliability.

3. **Customer Satisfaction:** The CSAT percentage is 84.9%. Comparing customer satisfaction with delivery performance could help identify whether delays are affecting customer experience.

4. **Hub Performance:** Houston Hub processes the highest order volume among the six hubs, with 380 orders shown in the hub overview. El Paso Hub processes 150 orders. Comparing order volumes with capacity and utilization can help identify workload imbalances.

5. **Hub Efficiency:** El Paso Hub has the highest displayed hub performance score of 0.83, while Fort Worth Hub has the lowest score of 0.57. These differences provide an opportunity to investigate operational practices and performance gaps.

6. **Driver Analysis:** The dashboard compares driver experience and performance ratings and identifies drivers with the most deliveries. These metrics can support further investigation of workload distribution and driver performance.

7. **Vehicle Maintenance:** The dashboard shows 537 total breakdowns, comprising 350 associated with active vehicles and 187 associated with vehicles in maintenance. The breakdown chart can help prioritize vehicle models for further investigation.

8. **Vehicle Orders:** Freightliner M2 has the highest displayed order count among vehicle models, with 220 orders, followed by Freightliner at 212 orders. This information can help evaluate vehicle workload and operational dependence.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- **Improve On-Time Deliveries:** Investigate delayed shipments, delivery routes, and hub processing times to identify the main causes of late deliveries.
- **Optimize Hub Operations:** Review workload and capacity at high-volume hubs and compare practices across hubs with different performance scores.
- **Monitor Driver Performance:** Use delivery counts, experience, and performance ratings together to identify training needs and review workload distribution.
- **Strengthen Vehicle Maintenance:** Investigate breakdown patterns by vehicle model and maintenance status to prioritize preventive maintenance.
- **Improve Customer Satisfaction:** Compare CSAT with delivery delays and other service indicators to identify opportunities to improve customer experience.
- **Track KPIs Regularly:** Monitor order volume, delivery performance, customer satisfaction, and breakdown counts over time to evaluate improvements.
